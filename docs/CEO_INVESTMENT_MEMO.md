## PART II — THE UNSOLVED PROBLEM & WEDGE

### 3. Exact Problem
Mid-market generic pharmaceutical manufacturers (turnover $20M–$300M in India, China, Vietnam, and Turkey) export 15 to 80 formulation products across 10 to 30 fragmented international markets (e.g., Kenya PPB, Tanzania TMDA, Uganda NDA, Nigeria NAFDAC, Ghana FDA, South Africa SAHPRA, Saudi SFDA, Vietnam DAV). 

When filing for market authorization across multiple jurisdictions, the Regulatory Affairs (RA) team must manually adapt the core Common Technical Document (CTD/ACTD Modules 1 through 5). Despite international templates, local NMRAs impose non-harmonized Module 1 administrative requirements, specific stability testing rules (Climate Zone IVa vs IVb), localized bioequivalence comparator specifications, and distinct CoPP requirements. 

Furthermore, NMRAs are implementing **Regulatory Reliance Frameworks** (WHO Listed Authority [WLA] framework, SADC Zazibona, East African Community MRH, ECOWAS Joint Assessment), allowing fast-tracked approvals if a product is already registered by a reference authority. However, RA teams lack software to automatically identify when a product qualifies for reliance, resulting in redundant 18-to-36-month submission cycles and $30,000–$80,000 in excess local consulting and re-testing costs per product per market.
